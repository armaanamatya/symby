### **TL;DR**

Machine learning is transforming science, but **how much, where, and with what consequences?**

Some claim AI is revolutionizing drug discovery and materials science. Others worry it's creating unreplicable results and citation bubbles. The truth is: **we don't actually know the quantitative impact.**

### Your challenge:

Build systems that measure ML's real influence on scientific progress across disciplines.

### **Dataset**

**81,000 scientific papers (2016-2024) and their metadata,** spanning computer science and domain sciences:

- 1,000+ ML/AI methodology papers (deep learning, transformers, GANs, RL techniques)
- 80,000 domain science papers across drug discovery, materials science, climate science, physics, neuroscience, and social sciences
- Rich metadata including citation networks, ML adoption markers (frameworks, compute resources, datasets referenced), reproducibility indicators (code/data availability, replication attempts), research outcomes (retractions, corrections, clinical trial progressions, patents), and impact metrics (citations, media coverage, policy influence)
- Temporal data tracking adoption curves and pre/post major ML breakthroughs (AlexNet, AlphaFold, GPT models)

### **Insight Goal**

Quantify machine learning's real impact on scientific progress across disciplines. Measure discovery acceleration, adoption patterns, reproducibility effects, and discipline-specific outcomes. Distinguish transformational ML applications from hype. Identify which fields benefit most, which ML techniques have staying power, and whether AI-enabled research is more or less reproducible than traditional methods.

### **Build Objective**

Create an interactive research intelligence platform that:

- **Quantifies ML impact** with attribution scoring (what % of breakthrough comes from ML vs. domain insight), acceleration metrics (did ML speed discovery by months/years), and efficiency measures (cost per discovery)
- **Visualizes adoption dynamics** across disciplines with interactive dashboards showing S-curves, citation flows from ML methods to domain papers, and animated temporal evolution (2016-2024)
- **Analyzes quality trade-offs** by cross-referencing ML adoption with reproducibility rates, retraction patterns/signals
- **Traces discovery impact** for high-profile cases (AlphaFold, COVID drug discovery) from ML method → domain application
