TL;DR
Machine learning is transforming science, but how much, where, and with what consequences?
Some claim AI is revolutionizing drug discovery and materials science. Others worry it's creating unreplicable results and citation bubbles. The truth is: we don't actually know the quantitative impact.
Your challenge: Build systems that measure ML's real influence on scientific progress across disciplines.

Insight Goal
Quantify machine learning's real impact on scientific progress across disciplines. Measure discovery acceleration, adoption patterns, reproducibility effects, and discipline-specific outcomes.
Key objectives:
Distinguish transformational ML applications from hype
Identify which fields benefit most
Determine which ML techniques have staying power
Assess whether AI-enabled research is more or less reproducible than traditional methods

Build Objective
Create an interactive research intelligence platform that includes one or more of these capabilities:
1. Quantify ML Impact
Measure how much ML actually contributes to scientific breakthroughs and discovery efficiency.
Attribution scoring: What % of breakthrough comes from ML vs. domain insight?
Acceleration metrics: Did ML speed discovery by months/years?
Efficiency measures: Cost per discovery
2. Visualize Adoption Dynamics
Show how ML techniques spread across scientific disciplines over time.
Interactive dashboards showing S-curves and citation flows from ML methods to domain papers
Animated temporal evolution (2016-2024)
Cross-discipline comparison views
3. Analyze Quality Trade-offs
Investigate whether ML adoption correlates with better or worse research reproducibility.
Cross-reference ML adoption with reproducibility rates
Retraction pattern detection and signals
Code/data availability correlations
4. Trace Discovery Impact
Follow the path from ML method development to real-world scientific impact.
High-profile case studies (AlphaFold, COVID drug discovery)
Journey tracking: ML method → domain application → real-world impact

Evaluation Criteria
Submissions will be evaluated on:
Impact measurement accuracy: How well do your metrics align with expected results in the validation dataset?
Insight depth: Does your analysis reveal novel, actionable insights about ML's impact on science?
Visualization quality: Are adoption dynamics and patterns clearly communicated?
Technical rigor: Is your methodology sound and reproducible?
Scalability: Can your approach handle the full 20M+ paper dataset?
